# Dimension Raid Phase 1 team recommendation

**Reviewed:** October 24, 2026

**Account reference:** JASWA Helper — roster overview imported October 23, 2026

**Scope:** Five different elements, with no monster repeated from the current
Phase 2 or Phase 3 presets. Current rune quality and placement were ignored.
This is a team to test, not a verified clear.

## First team to test

Try **Amarna (leader), Hraesvelg, Hellea, Shaina #1 and Sabrina**. It meets the
one-monster-per-element criterion and none of these monsters appears in the
shown Phase 2 or Phase 3 teams. The strongest reason to pair Shaina with
Sabrina is their Chakram Dancer / Boomerang Warrior team-up; the rest of the
team adds a speed-and-Attack buff, Brand and Defense break, and team recovery.

| Monster | Element | Job in this team | Why it earns the slot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shaina #1** | Fire | Multi-hit setup and team-up damage | Her passive lowers Defense with each attack. Her Chakram Dancer attacks with a Boomerang Warrior during that ally's turn. |
| **Sabrina** | Water | Multi-hit damage and team-up partner | Her S1 can call a Chakram Dancer for a joint attack; her S2 hits three times and can lower Defense and Attack. Her passive also shares its damage and mitigation effect with a Chakram Dancer on the team. |
| **Hraesvelg** | Wind | Opening team buff, Brand and damage | His S3 raises team Attack and Attack Speed for two turns. It then puts him into Berserk, which increases his damage and speed but lowers his Defense. |
| **Amarna** | Light | Cleanse, healing and recovery | His S3 removes harmful effects from the whole team and heals it; it can revive fallen allies. His other skills offer Defense break and Brand. |
| **Hellea** | Dark | Brand and Defense break | Her awakened S3 applies both to the target. Her S1 is another two-hit Defense-break chance. |

Shaina and Sabrina are the deliberate pair here: one is a Chakram Dancer and
the other a Boomerang Warrior, so each can call the other into a team attack
from her own turn. **Talia #1 is a Water Chakram Dancer**, not a Boomerang
Warrior. Replacing Sabrina with Talia keeps the element distinct but removes
that family pairing unless another Boomerang Warrior is added; adding Maruna
would duplicate Shaina's Fire element. [Shaina's skills](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/21902-fire-chakram-dancer-shaina/),
[Sabrina's skills](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/22001-water-boomerang-warrior-sabrina/),
[Talia's skills and family](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/21901-water-chakram-dancer-talia/)

The current Phase 2 preset shows Icaru (2A) #1, Kro (2A), Raoq (2A) #1, Fran
and Loren. Phase 3 shows Carcano #1, Darion (2A), Veromos, Riley #1 and Laika.
The recommended five are separate from both lists. Com2uS requires five
monsters for each phase and says duplicate monsters cannot be used across the
teams. [Dimension Raid rules and rotating Phase 1 condition](https://summonerswar.com/en/skyarena/news/list/4419?category=update&page=1)

## Leader choice

Use **Amarna's +25% Defense leader skill** for the first stability test. It is
a General lead, so it reaches all five monsters in Dimension Raid under
JASWA's Raid rule (`SPEED-TICK-CANONICAL.md` §2.2). Hellea's +30% Attack lead
only reaches Hellea; Shaina's Dungeon lead and Sabrina's and Hraesvelg's Guild
leads do not count in this content. [Amarna's skills and leader skill](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/20404-light-anubis-amarna/),
[Hellea's skills and leader skill](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/10505-dark-harpy-hellea/)

## Opening and build priorities

Use JASWA's Speed Tuning view to give **Hraesvelg an early turn** for his S3
team buff. Put Hellea or Amarna ahead of the main damage turns when their Brand
or Defense break is needed. Keep Amarna fast and durable enough to cover the
team; Hraesvelg needs extra bulk because Berserk lowers his Defense. Aim for
Shaina and Sabrina to use their joint attacks when their AI chooses their
single-target skills. Auto Battle may choose another skill, so confirm the
opening and team-up frequency in a real run. The rune assignments in the
account snapshot did not affect this recommendation.

Hellea and Amarna both offer Brand and Defense break, so their debuffs overlap.
Keep both initially for the five-element lineup and Amarna's recovery, then
check which Brand/Defense-break windows the boss actually needs. Do not count
every listed skill effect as guaranteed: the monster skills have activation
chances, and the boss may resist or be immune to particular effects. The
Dimension Raid announcement does not provide its boss skills as searchable
text, so validate the result in the current Phase 1.

## Entry condition

The Phase 1 entry condition follows the active Dimension Dungeon and rotates;
Phases 2 and 3 have fixed conditions. These five are default 3- or 4-star
monsters, so they fit a maximum-default-4-star condition, but they do not fit a
restriction that excludes default 4-star monsters. Check the live Phase 1
screen before moving them. [Com2uS on the three phases and changing Phase 1 condition](https://summonerswar.com/en/skyarena/news/list/4419?category=update&page=1)

## First-run check

Start with Amarna as leader and observe one manual run:

1. Confirm the five elements and verify all five meet the current entry
   condition.
2. Check whether Hraesvelg uses S3 early enough for the team buff to matter.
3. Watch Shaina and Sabrina's turns for their team-up attacks; note when Auto
   chooses skills other than their single-target attack.
4. Check whether Brand and Defense break land before the main damage turns,
   and whether Amarna's heal/cleanse is enough to keep the team stable.

Keep the team provisional until the actual Phase 1 run confirms its survival,
damage and opening order. If the current entry restriction excludes default
4-star monsters, choose another team for that week while preserving the
different-element and no-Phase-2/3-overlap criteria.
