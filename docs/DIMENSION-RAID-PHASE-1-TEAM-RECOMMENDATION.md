# Dimension Raid Phase 1 team recommendation

**Reviewed:** October 24, 2026

**Account reference:** JASWA Helper — roster overview imported October 23, 2026

**Scope:** One Phase 1 team, subject to the live weekly entry condition and the
rule that a monster cannot be used in more than one phase. Rune assignments are
out of scope; this is a team to test, not a verified clear.

## First team to test

Use **Jultan (2A), Vigor (2A), Naomi (2A), Kaki and Amarna (leader)**. The
opening aims to put four monsters ahead of the boss, then let Amarna act just
after its first turn so he can cleanse and heal the team. Vigor's heal and
Attack Speed buff provide follow-up sustain. [Com2uS confirms that Phase 1
uses the previous Dimension Predator as its boss](https://summonerswar.com/en/skyarena/news/list/4419?category=update&page=1).

| Order | Monster | Element | Job in this team |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Jultan (2A)** | Dark | Open with a single-target attack. His passive lowers Defense with each attack; his high-HP passive also returns damage when the boss critically hits him. |
| 2 | **Vigor (2A)** | Water | Use S3 to add three Defense-break chances before Naomi attacks. Save S2's team heal and Speed buff until after the boss has taken its first turn. |
| 3 | **Naomi (2A)** | Wind | Attack after Jultan and Vigor have had chances to apply harmful effects. Her passive gains guaranteed Critical Rate against debuffed targets and more damage per debuff. |
| 4 | **Kaki** | Fire | Add damage before the boss moves; his S1 heals him, and his S2 gives him area damage for the Soul Clones. |
| 5 | **Amarna (leader)** | Light | Follow the boss's opening attack with S3 to remove all harmful effects from the team, heal, and revive anyone defeated. |

Jultan's passive can apply Defense break before Naomi, while Vigor's S3 adds
more chances for it. Naomi's Attack Bar reduction does not work on the boss,
which is immune to that effect. Kaki cannot land Critical Hits and converts
Attack into Defense, giving the team another sturdy attacker. [Jultan's 2A
skills](https://swarena.gg/monster/jultan-2a), [Vigor's 2A
skills](https://swarena.gg/monster/vigor-2a), [Naomi's 2A
skills](https://rosa-midman.com/game-data/summoners-war-rta/monster/naomi-2a),
[Kaki's skills](https://swarena.gg/monster/kaki)

The boss's **Endless Pain** inflicts two Continuous Damage effects with no
turn limit. Amarna's S3 removes the whole team's harmful effects and heals for
30% of his Max HP, making his post-boss turn useful even if the DoTs land.
Vigor's S2 then provides another team heal (20%), Speed buff and reduced chance
of receiving Critical Hits. [Amarna's skills](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/20404-light-anubis-amarna/),
[Dimension Hole boss guide](https://theriagames.com/guide/summoners-war-dimensional-hole/)

The current Phase 2 and Phase 3 presets shown in the account reference do not
reserve Jultan, Vigor, Naomi, Kaki or Amarna. The entry condition for Phase 1
rotates with the active Dimension Dungeon, so check the live raid screen after
each rotation. These five fit a maximum-default-4-star restriction; they do not
fit one that excludes default 4-star monsters. [Com2uS on phase teams and the
rotating entry condition](https://summonerswar.com/en/skyarena/news/list/4419?category=update&page=1)

## Leader choice

Use **Amarna's General +25% Defense lead** for stability. General leads count
in Dimension Raid under JASWA's Raid rule (SPEED-TICK-CANONICAL.md §2.2).
Jultan's and Vigor's Guild leads, and Naomi's Guild Attack Speed lead, do not
count in this content. Amarna can lead while taking the last turn; leader
choice does not determine move order. [Amarna's leader skill](https://swarfarm.com/bestiary/20404-light-anubis-amarna/)

## Speed and turn order

Set the first four combat speeds near your proposed **215 / 210 / 205 / 200**.
The order below puts Jultan and Vigor's Defense-break setup before Naomi's
damage, then Kaki before the boss. Keep Amarna around **145 combat speed** if
you want him to act just after the boss's first turn and respond with S3. The
boss's speed of **150** is a community report, not an official Com2uS figure or
a JASWA enemy record; confirm the actual opening order in battle.

| Opening order | Monster | Combat speed target | Approx. display speed with level-20 Totem | First-turn plan |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | **Jultan (2A)** | **215** | **197** | Apply a harmful effect and Defense-break pressure. |
| 2 | **Vigor (2A)** | **210** | **192** | Use S3 for more Defense-break chances; do not use S2 yet if Amarna is meant to move after the boss. |
| 3 | **Naomi (2A)** | **205** | **187** | Deal damage after the setup. |
| 4 | **Kaki** | **200** | **182** | Attack the boss; switch target priority to the Soul Clones when they appear. |
| 5 | **Amarna (leader)** | **145** | **129** | Move just after a 150-speed boss; use S3 to cleanse and heal after its opening attack. |

The display-speed estimates assume a level-20 Sky Tribe Totem (+15%), Amarna's
Defense lead, and no Swift set. They use awakened base speeds Jultan 115,
Vigor 115, Naomi 119, Kaki 115 and Amarna 101. They are approximate; use the
combat-speed targets in JASWA Speed Tuning for the finished rune builds. Vigor's
S2 increases all allies' combat speed by 30% for two turns before any
recipient's SPD UP Effect from artifacts. If used before Amarna's first turn,
it can pull him ahead of the boss, so hold S2 until the boss has acted when
following this slower-Amarna plan. If you instead want Vigor to open with S2,
raise Amarna to around **190–200 combat speed** and save his S3 for his next
turn after the boss's first attack. [Community report for the
150-speed reference](https://www.reddit.com/r/summonerswar/comments/183g8ik/dimensional_raid_1_team_4_dimensions/)

All four fast members are comfortably above the reported boss speed, leaving
rune quality for HP and Defense. Keep Jultan bulky to benefit from his passive;
prioritize survival on Naomi and Kaki if either drops low. Amarna's heal scales
with his Max HP, so build him for HP, Defense and enough speed to land near the
chosen timing. Vigor also wants HP and Defense so the team support survives to
use S2. These are opening speeds; Vigor's later Speed buff changes the order
and can give allies extra turns.

Set the game's **Attack Order** separately from Speed: target the boss in the
opening, then switch to the Soul Clones when they appear. Return to the boss
after they are defeated. [Com2uS on Attack Order](https://summonerswar.com/en/skyarena/news/list/4420)

## First-run check

Try one manual run before relying on Repeat Battle:

1. Confirm Jultan, Vigor, Naomi and Kaki all move before the boss.
2. If Amarna is at 145, confirm the boss moves before Amarna and use Amarna's
   S3 immediately after the boss's opening attack.
3. Hold Vigor's S2 until after that boss turn if you want Amarna to stay behind
   it; otherwise his Speed buff can change the plan.
4. Check how much HP remains after the DoTs, whether Amarna's cleanse and heal
   stabilize the team, and whether Kaki clears the Soul Clones in time.

If the boss's real speed or starting Attack Bar differs from the community
report, tune Amarna from the observed turn order. If anyone still drops into
danger, add bulk before pushing the four opening speeds higher. This remains a
recommendation to test, not a verified clear.
