# Solo R5 speed-team recommendation

**Account snapshot:** JASWA Helper — Steam, imported October 20, 2026

**Scope:** Three distinct six-monster teams for one Solo Rift Raid 5 run. No team uses a planned death or revive loop.

## Recommendation at a glance

| Team | Leader | Main idea | Frontline / backline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Theomars | Miriam-amplified buffs, Brandia and Baleygr burst | 4 / 2 |
| 2 | Tesarion | Resistance lead, Dias mitigation, Hwa control, twin damage | 2 / 4 |
| 3 | Akhamamir | Raid-wide speed lead, cleanse/brand, Icaru–Raoq team-ups | 4 / 2 |

All 18 selected monsters are in the imported account and are six-star, level 40. The teams are built for simultaneous use, so keep their rune assignments disjoint during the raid. Runes can be moved between presets before or after the run; one rune cannot serve two of these teams at once.

This is a speed-oriented recommendation with a safety margin, not a claim that the exact 18 builds have already been solved rune-by-rune. The import contains 1,443 six-star runes; JASWA's provisional rune-efficiency estimate has a median of 92 and a 25th percentile of 81. That is a healthy pool, but a set label is never worth weakening a monster's required EHP or speed. The estimate is only a sorting aid, not an official game score.

The R5 guide's starting survival marks are 25k HP / 1.5k DEF for a two-monster frontline, 22k / 1.3k for a four-monster frontline, and 20k / 800 DEF for the backline. I add a practical margin below those marks because the stated goal is to avoid intentional deaths and keep the teams reliable. The boss's frontline Crush ignores beneficial effects, so Will is not a substitute for bulk. [SWMasters R5 guide](https://www.swmasters.com/blog/r5-guide), [SW Database R5 guide](https://www.sw-database.com/post/r5-beginners-guide)

## Speed tuning

The R5 boss is commonly listed at 192 SPD, 30% RES and 0% ACC. The table below uses the account's level-20 Sky Tribe Totem (+15%) and the listed leader. Speeds in the team columns are **display SPD**: the figure on the monster panel after runes, including any Swift-set bonus. The combat-speed column is an estimate for a non-Swift set; Swift rounding can move a displayed tune by one point. The six-point or eight-point spacing is intended to preserve the order. Recheck the final engraved runes with JASWA's Raid speed tuner, which applies the exact speed-tick rules. [R5 boss stats](https://theriagames.com/guide/summoners-war-chaos-beast-guide/), [JASWA speed rules](SPEED-TICK-CANONICAL.md)

The R5 clock advances in 4.5% ticks. The boss's three heads share attack gauge at battle start, so exceeding its normal 192 combat SPD gives the team a useful tempo edge but does not promise that no head can take an opening action. These targets keep every monster above the boss's normal speed after the opening state; the boss's shared gauge, debuffs and attack timing are why the EHP and cleanse requirements still matter. [SWMasters boss mechanics](https://www.swmasters.com/blog/r5-guide)

## Shared stat and rune rules

- **EHP and raw floors:** Use JASWA's `HP × (1142 + 3.572 × DEF) / 1000`. Treat 130k / 162.5k / 80k as rough guide EHP floors for four-front / two-front / backline. The tables aim higher: 140k / 175k / 90k. Also keep the guide's raw marks: 22k HP / 1.3k DEF for front-4, 25k / 1.5k for front-2, and 20k / 800 DEF for backline. Do not satisfy EHP with very low DEF: the boss's frontline Crush punishes low Defense. If one monster misses a target, give it bulk or move a fourth unit to the frontline before spending more damage stats. [JASWA calculation authority](CALCULATION-AUTHORITY.md)
- **ACC:** Aim for 15% total accuracy on monsters that must land debuffs. Do not keep adding ACC past that for this boss. Pure buff/heal units can leave ACC at zero if their attacks do not supply a required debuff.
- **RES:** Aim for 70% panel RES on frontliners, healers and cleansers when there is no RES lead. Under Tesarion, 29% panel RES plus his +41% lead is the comparable target. Damage dealers may trade excess RES for damage after reaching their EHP requirement.
- **Critical Rate:** Team 1 aims for 76% panel CR under Theomars's +24% general lead. Teams 2 and 3 aim for 100% panel CR on damage dealers. Awakening bonuses are already part of the monster's panel stat. Supports that do not rely on critical damage do not need 100% CR.
- **Sets:** Rage / Will is the first choice for the main damage dealers, not a command to accept weak Rage runes. Use a strong Fatal / Will, Vampire / Will or other stat-efficient build if it meets the speed, EHP and damage role better. Use Swift for the first movers only when the final displayed SPD matches the table. Use Will as a useful opening safeguard, but never at the cost of frontline EHP.
- **Do not make three teams share a rune.** Solo R5 uses the three teams in one run. The account has ample Will runes; Rage is more limited, so reserve its best four-set combinations for Baleygr, Brandia and the highest-output damage dealers first.
- **Skill-ups:** Brandia currently has no skill-ups and should be max-skilled before receiving the account's best Rage set. Baleygr and Colleen (2A) are also incomplete; finish their important damage/heal skill-ups. Incomplete skill-ups make damage or debuff uptime less dependable.

## Candidates considered but not selected

- **Loren:** Hwa is preferred in Team 2 because she combines attack-speed slow, ATB reduction and damage. Loren is a reasonable swap for Talia only if the team needs more control; it loses the twin damage pairing. She does not replace Riley's cleanse.
- **Tarq:** His team-up can add damage, and SWGT includes him in Solo R5 examples. I kept the Icaru–Raoq pair and Hellea's branding/DEF break rather than adding a third team-up unit; this leaves the third team with a clearer damage/debuff mix. If using Tarq, test him in Raoq's slot and recheck EHP, CR and the resulting run consistency. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1)
- **Shihwa:** A good R5 brand/damage candidate. Hellea is selected because she is already fully skilled, while Shihwa's account skill-ups are incomplete; Shihwa can replace Hellea after skill-ups if her final EHP and speed are better. [Shihwa R5 rating](https://www.sw-database.com/shihwa)
- **Lucas:** A legitimate beginner-R5 option with a self-generated shield, brand and conditional DEF break. He is not in the final 18 because Team 3 already has brand/DEF break from Amarna and Hellea and benefits from keeping the Icaru–Raoq pair. Consider Lucas in place of Hellea only if he reaches the same backline EHP and 172 display SPD; his S2 needs CR to make the DEF break dependable. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1), [Lucas skills](https://swgt.io/monsterSearch/?ss=Lucas&usePublic=false)
- **Tien Qin:** His heal, anti-crit buff, ATK break and heal block are useful in a sustain team, but he has no cleanse and his +16% SPD leader is Guild-only, so it does not lead an R5 team. Riley already covers the safer cleanse/heal role in Team 2. [Tien Qin guide and leader area](https://theriagames.com/guide/summoners-war-tien-qin-light-drunken-master/)
- **Astar:** A viable damage alternative that appears in Solo R5 team examples. I did not trade away Hellea's brand/DEF break for another mostly-damage slot; try Astar in Hellea's position only if he meets the same backline EHP and speed and the run still lands enough debuffs. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1)
- **Ken:** Strong damage in a debuff-heavy fight, but Hellea supplies more reliable branding/DEF break to this particular Akhamamir team. Ken is a direct Hellea alternative if his actual rune build and damage are stronger.
