# Solo R5 speed-team recommendation

**Account snapshot:** JASWA Helper — Steam, imported October 20, 2026

**Scope:** Three distinct six-monster teams for one Solo Rift Raid 5 run. No team uses a planned death or revive loop.

## Recommendation at a glance

  | Team | Leader    | Main idea                                                  | Frontline / backline |
  | ---- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
  | 1    | Theomars  | Miriam-amplified buffs, Brandia and Baleygr burst          | 4 / 2                |
  | 2    | Tesarion  | Resistance lead, Dias mitigation, Hwa control, twin damage | 2 / 4                |
  | 3    | Akhamamir | Raid-wide speed lead, cleanse/brand, Icaru–Raoq team-ups   | 4 / 2                |

All 18 selected monsters are in the imported account and are six-star, level 40. The teams are built for simultaneous use, so keep their rune assignments disjoint during the raid. Runes can be moved between presets before or after the run; one rune cannot serve two of these teams at once.

**The three leader skills are shared across all 18 monsters in R5.** Select Theomars, Tesarion and Akhamamir as the three team leaders: their distinct general leads give the whole raid +24% Critical Rate, +41% Resistance and +24% Attack Speed. The three bonuses stack because they affect different stats. Raid leaders are chosen one per team; they are not team-local. Ally buffs such as Miriam's passive-enhanced buffs remain within the six-monster team that casts them, while harmful effects on the boss are shared across the raid. [Com2uS raid announcement](https://m-mercury.qpyou.cn/custom/board_detail/4), [Rift of Worlds guide](https://summonerswarskyarena.info/rift-of-worlds-analysis-and-basic-guide-by-xelnecra/)

This is a speed-oriented recommendation with a safety margin, not a claim that the exact 18 builds have already been solved rune-by-rune. The current import contains 1,445 six-star runes; JASWA's provisional rune-efficiency estimate has a median of 92 and a 25th percentile of 81. That is a healthy pool, but a set label is never worth weakening a monster's required EHP or speed. The estimate is only a sorting aid, not an official game score.

The R5 guide's starting survival marks are 25k HP / 1.5k DEF for a two-monster frontline, 22k / 1.3k for a four-monster frontline, and 20k / 800 DEF for the backline. I add a practical margin below those marks because the stated goal is to avoid intentional deaths and keep the teams reliable. The boss's frontline Crush ignores beneficial effects, so Will is not a substitute for bulk. [SWMasters R5 guide](https://www.swmasters.com/blog/r5-guide), [SW Database R5 guide](https://www.sw-database.com/post/r5-beginners-guide)

## Speed tuning

The R5 boss is commonly listed at 192 combat SPD, 30% RES and 0% ACC. Each head has its own attack bar and faces one six-monster team; the boss's HP and harmful effects are shared. The targets below use the account's level-20 Sky Tribe Totem (+15%) and Akhamamir's shared +24% Attack Speed lead for every team. They preserve the original combat-speed tiers while reducing the display-SPD rune burden on Teams 1 and 2. Speeds in the team columns are **display SPD**: the figure on the monster panel after runes, including any Swift-set bonus. Where a Swift set changes the minimum panel figure by one point, the table gives the Swift target first and the non-Swift figure in parentheses. Combat SPD is calculated with the exact raid formula for the listed base SPD and rune set; recheck final engraved runes with JASWA's Raid speed tuner. [R5 boss stats](https://theriagames.com/guide/summoners-war-chaos-beast-guide/), [Com2uS raid mechanics](https://m-mercury.qpyou.cn/custom/board_detail/4), [JASWA speed rules](SPEED-TICK-CANONICAL.md)

The R5 clock advances in 4.5% ticks. Every monster below is tuned above the boss's 192 combat SPD, with each team's last mover at 197 or faster. This gives every team a first-turn margin against its own head; it does not replace the EHP and cleanse requirements or guarantee that a run cannot fail to RNG. [SWMasters boss mechanics](https://www.swmasters.com/blog/r5-guide)

## Shared stat and rune rules

- **EHP and raw floors:** Use JASWA's `HP × (1142 + 3.572 × DEF) / 1000`. Treat 130k / 162.5k / 80k as rough guide EHP floors for four-front / two-front / backline. The tables aim higher: 140k / 175k / 90k. Also keep the guide's raw marks: 22k HP / 1.3k DEF for front-4, 25k / 1.5k for front-2, and 20k / 800 DEF for backline. Do not satisfy EHP with very low DEF: the boss's frontline Crush punishes low Defense. If one monster misses a target, give it bulk or move a fourth unit to the frontline before spending more damage stats. [JASWA calculation authority](CALCULATION-AUTHORITY.md)
- **ACC:** Aim for 15% total panel accuracy on monsters that must land debuffs. Awakened bonuses count toward the total: Hwa, Tesarion and Loren already exceed it and need no rune ACC. Do not keep adding ACC past 15% for this boss. Pure buff/heal units can leave ACC at zero if their attacks do not supply a required debuff.
- **RES:** All 18 monsters receive Tesarion's shared +41% RES lead. Aim for 29% panel RES (about 70% in battle) on frontline monsters, healers and cleansers. Damage dealers may trade RES for damage after reaching their EHP requirement.
- **Critical Rate:** All 18 monsters receive Theomars's shared +24% CR lead. Damage dealers target 76% panel CR; awakening bonuses are already part of the monster's panel stat. Supports that do not rely on critical damage do not need 100% CR.
- **SPD:** Every speed target includes Akhamamir's shared +24% Attack Speed lead and the level-20 +15% totem. Match the exact preferred-set display target in the tables, including the stated Swift adjustment, then verify all six members remain above their own head's 192 combat SPD in JASWA's raid tuner.
- **Sets:** Rage / Will is the first choice for the main damage dealers, not a command to accept weak Rage runes. Use a strong Fatal / Will, Vampire / Will or other stat-efficient build if it meets the speed, EHP and damage role better. Use Swift for first movers only when it is the best stat-efficient way to meet the exact target. Use Will as a useful opening safeguard, but never at the cost of frontline EHP.
- **Do not make three teams share a rune.** Solo R5 uses the three teams in one run. The account has ample Will runes; Rage is more limited, so reserve its best four-set combinations for Baleygr, Brandia and the highest-output damage dealers first.
- **Skill-ups:** Brandia currently has no skill-ups and should be max-skilled before receiving the account's best Rage set. Baleygr and Colleen (2A) are also incomplete; finish their important damage/heal skill-ups. Incomplete skill-ups make damage or debuff uptime less dependable.

## Candidates considered but not selected

- **Hellea:** She is a strong alternative to Loren if you prefer direct damage, DEF break and branding over extra S1 attack-bar control. She is fully skilled in this account; use her at Loren's backline position with 199 display SPD so she moves before Icaru, Raoq and Akhamamir. [Hellea skills](https://summonersky.com/monster/dark-harpy)
- **Tarq:** His team-up can add damage, and SWGT includes him in Solo R5 examples. I kept the Icaru–Raoq pair and Loren's S1 control rather than adding a third team-up unit; this leaves room for boss control and Akhamamir's damage. If using Tarq, test him in Raoq's slot and recheck EHP, CR and the resulting run consistency. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1)
- **Shihwa:** A good R5 brand/damage candidate. Loren is selected for the extra S1 boss control in the team-up package; Shihwa can replace Loren if you prefer more damage and brand uptime and her final EHP and speed are better. [Shihwa R5 rating](https://www.sw-database.com/shihwa)
- **Lucas:** A legitimate beginner-R5 option with a self-generated shield, brand and conditional DEF break. Team 3 keeps Loren's S1 control and the Icaru–Raoq pair. Consider Lucas only if you are willing to drop one of those roles and he meets the same backline EHP and speed target; his S2 needs CR to make the DEF break dependable. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1), [Lucas skills](https://swgt.io/monsterSearch/?ss=Lucas&usePublic=false)
- **Tien Qin:** His heal, anti-crit buff, ATK break and heal block are useful in a sustain team, but he has no cleanse and his +16% SPD leader is Guild-only, so it does not lead an R5 team. Riley already covers the safer cleanse/heal role in Team 2. [Tien Qin guide and leader area](https://theriagames.com/guide/summoners-war-tien-qin-light-drunken-master/)
- **Astar:** A viable damage alternative that appears in Solo R5 team examples. He trades away Loren's team-up S1 control; try him in Loren's position only if he meets the backline EHP and 200 display SPD target and the run still controls the boss reliably. [SWGT Solo RAID examples](https://swgt.io/controllers/gameguides/soloRAID?raidID=1)
- **Ken:** Strong damage in a debuff-heavy fight, but Loren adds repeated ATB reduction and DEF-break attempts through the team-up package. Ken is a direct Loren alternative if your run needs more damage and his build clears the same backline survival and speed checks.
